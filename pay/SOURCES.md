# Payment acceptance marks

Official artwork, unaltered. Each brand requires that you use the file they
publish and do not redraw or recolour it, so these are copied in byte for byte
and sized only with CSS.

| `google-pay-mark.svg` | `developers.google.com/static/pay/api/download-assets/Google-Pay-Acceptance.zip` → `Google Pay Mark/google-pay-mark_800.svg` | 22 Sep 2026 |
| `paypal-mark.svg` | `paypalobjects.com/paypal-ui/logos/svg/paypal-color.svg` | 22 Sep 2026 |

Rules that constrain how they are used on the page:

- **Apple** — use Apple's artwork only, never a redrawn version. Clear space of
  at least a quarter of the mark's height on every side.
- **Google** — never alter the mark, never translate the word "Pay", and never
  show it smaller than the other payment marks beside it. Clear space of half
  the height of the capital G.
- **PayPal** — original colours only, unmodified.

Re-check the source files annually; brands reissue them.
