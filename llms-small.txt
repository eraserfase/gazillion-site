# Gazillion Industries

> Audio plug-ins for macOS and Windows: AU, VST3 and standalone. Each does one
> job and needs no manual. Tuned by ear; none is an emulation of any hardware.

Last updated: 2026-09-25

## Products

- [BEEFY](https://gazillionindustries.com/beefy.html): Saturation and loudness for Mac and Windows. AU, VST3 and standalone.
- [DRUGS](https://gazillionindustries.com/drugs.html): A one-knob bus compressor for drums. Free, Mac and Windows.
- [F(ilter)12](https://gazillionindustries.com/f12.html): A 12 bit sauce filter for Mac and Windows. AU, VST3 and standalone.
- [SKRUU](https://gazillionindustries.com/skruu.html): Varispeed pitch and time stretch for Mac and Windows.
- [TRIPLE OG](https://gazillionindustries.com/tripleog.html): Cassette transport for Mac and Windows. AU, VST3 and standalone.
