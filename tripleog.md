## What TRIPLE OG is

A cassette transport. Six controls for what the medium is and what it is doing, and one for your hand.

Most cassette sims and lofi tape plug-ins have wow and flutter. Very few have a transport that actually loses its footing, banks the time, and then sprints to rejoin the live stream. That behavior is the point of this one, and stacking stock plug-ins does not get to it at any setting.

## The tape

- TONE is the playback system's brightness. 50 is a musically neutral cassette, not a bypass.
- HISS is a continuous broadband bed, shaped and partly decorrelated across the channels. No clicks, no crackle, no dust — that is a different medium.
- AGE is in years, nought to sixty, because that is what the quantity is. Darker, noisier, slightly more saturated, less speed-stable and less perfectly matched left to right, all on one knob.
- DRIVE is soft asymmetric saturation, oversampled. Even harmonics dominate across the whole travel, and the level stays flat inside half a decibel from one end of the knob to the other — so it gets dirtier without getting louder.
- MIX is on the face and defaults to 100%. Nothing dry is blended in behind your back to flatter the transients.

## The transport

- WOW FLUT is continuous, bounded instability built from several motions at once. One sine is a chorus and everybody hears it.
- CATCH is the machine failing on its own: it drags, jams, and then recovers. It fires on its own clock, and there is a trigger on the panel for when you want one now.
- SCRUB is somebody doing it on purpose. Drag the lever left and the head runs back across the tape; let go and it rebounds home. While your hand is on it, it wins over any catch.
- SLAM is the tape stop. Hold it and the transport drags to a halt; let go and it comes back up to speed. SLAM RETURN sets how long the stop takes.

## The display

The reels, the tape, and the two heads. The outline is what you sent it, the fill is what came back, and the gap between them is the tape holding on. Above them a pair of VU meters against a 0 VU reference, so how hard you are hitting it is on the panel instead of in a manual.

## Latency

About 4 ms, and it is reported to the host, so delay compensation lines your mix back up.

## Formats and compatibility

On macOS, TRIPLE OG installs as an Audio Unit (AU) and a VST3 plug-in, plus a standalone application. macOS 10.13 or later, universal binary for both Apple Silicon and Intel.

On Windows it is a VST3. Windows 10 or later, 64-bit. It arrives as a zip you drop into your VST3 folder; there is no installer yet.

Any sample rate your session runs at. No internet connection required after activation.
