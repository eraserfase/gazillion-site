the lo-fi section.

SAMPLE RATE and BIT are the crush pair. SAMPLE RATE slows the rate the audio is sampled at, so the top goes and what was up there folds back down as new content. BIT coarsens the steps it is measured in, so quiet detail rounds off and what is left picks up a hard edge. Twelve bits is as clean as this gets. It only goes down from there.

the filter.

  • POSITION sets where in the spectrum the filter sits. The further it works the more it drives and the more it sings — built in, not something to dial in.
  • Five shapes: low-pass, high-pass, band-pass, notch, and a morph that runs from a closing low-pass, through untouched in the middle, to an opening high-pass.
  • SLOPE is how hard it falls away past the corner, from 18 dB per octave up to a wall where almost nothing gets past. DIRTY feeds the filter’s own distortion back through itself; off, the same filter runs clean.
  • Q SHAPE puts peaks and dips on the filter at fixed distances from wherever it is, so the whole shape travels with the knob. WAH is one strong peak at the cutoff, VOWEL is four peaks spaced like a voice, COMB alternates peaks and nulls, SCOOP lifts both ends and pulls the cutoff down between them. PLAIN adds nothing.
  • DRAW is your own. Click the display to add a point, drag it anywhere from silence to a big boost, pull its handles for width, and right-click it for a notch, a shelf or a cut. The points ride the cutoff like every other shape.
  • DRIVE, RESO, Q and MIX. RESO decides how much resonance; Q decides how narrow.

moving the filter.

Two ways, one at a time — engaging either releases the other, because both driving the same cutoff means neither knob does what it says.

AUTO FILTER hands it to a clock: a shape, a division, a grid, SWING to push it off the grid, and PHASE for where in the shape each cycle starts. SIDECHAIN hands it to a signal instead — what it follows, what it hears, which way it travels, how far, and how eagerly it makes up its mind.

the display.

The heavy line is the filter. Behind it is what is going in and what is coming out. The field of pixels is AURA, drawn honestly: green where it is adding, red where it is taking away, nothing where it has no opinion. It lives inside the filter, so past the cutoff there is none.

BYPASS takes the whole plug-in out. The panel goes gray and the display goes empty.
