### About WINNETKA

WINNETKA is a polysynth with a vocoder and 350 preloaded presets: bass, lead, pads, FX, percussion, arp and vocoder.

Play a single sound or layer two timbres. Shape your voice with the vocoder, or play the held-vowel presets without a microphone. The bank includes 40 vocoder presets, with formant and shift controls.

A 16-step arpeggiator with rates up to 1/64. Filters, envelopes, modulation, EQ and delay. Save your own presets, import new ones, share and trade on our discord!

AU, VST3 and standalone on macOS. VST3 on Windows. Intel and Apple Silicon Macs. macOS 10.13 or later; Windows 10 or later, 64-bit.
