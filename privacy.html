Privacy

What this site collects, why, and how to have it removed.

Gazillion Industries is a one-person company that makes audio plug-ins. Nothing here is sold, rented or shared with data brokers, and there is no advertising network running on this site beyond what is listed below.

What the site itself collects

The website measurement services are described below.

ServiceWhat it doesWhat it holds
Umami Counts page visits as an independent check of the site's activity. No cookies and no cross-site tracking. Page address, referrer, country, browser and device type, and an anonymous identifier that rotates monthly. It does not contain your name or email address.
Meta pixel Reports that a visit or a checkout happened, so advertising on Facebook and Instagram can be measured and targeted. Meta receives the page visited and, if a purchase is started, the product and price. Meta may connect that to your Facebook or Instagram account. This one is genuinely advertising tracking, and if you would rather it did not happen, a tracker blocker or Meta's own ad preferences will stop it.
Google Analytics Counts visits, where visitors came from and what they do, on these pages and on the Gumroad pages where the plug-ins are sold, so decisions about where to spend time and money can use recorded activity. Sets cookies. Google receives the page address, the page you came from, approximate location, browser and device type, and a random identifier kept in a cookie so that a returning visit can be recognized. Google explains how it uses that here. A tracker blocker or Google's opt-out add-on will stop it.
Gazillion activity Records page visits, source tags, the names (never the values) of link parameters, button presses and demo playback on our own server. It also shows which pages are currently open: visible pages send a presence signal every 15 seconds and a departure signal when hidden or closed. Presence renewals are not additional visits. No additional cookies or browser storage. Our server creates anonymous identifiers from the connection address and browser information using a private key; it does not retain the address or browser string. Records include browser and device categories, preferred language and location details supplied by our existing website infrastructure. Screen dimensions help classify the device; the dimensions are not retained. Live identifiers rotate daily and presence signals expire automatically. Recorded visit identifiers rotate monthly, and activity records are retained to preserve business history. Existing anonymous Umami, Google and journey identifiers can connect matching observations without counting them twice. An order carrying the same journey token can be linked to its observed website steps. These observations do not identify visits to Gumroad's payment form.

What happens when you buy something

Payment is handled entirely by Gumroad. Card details are never sent to this site and are never seen by Gazillion Industries. What comes back is your email address, the product, the amount, and the country the order came from, which is what a receipt and a license need. Gumroad's own privacy policy covers their side.

What happens if you join the email list

The list runs on Kit. It holds the email address you gave, and which emails you opened or clicked, which is how sending stays occasional rather than constant. Every email carries an unsubscribe link and it works immediately.

The plug-ins themselves

The plug-ins need an internet connection once, to activate. After that they do not phone home: they contain no analytics, send nothing anywhere, and work with no internet connection. What you make with them is yours and is never transmitted.

Removing your data

Email tony@gazillionindustries.com and say what you want removed. No form and no account required. Purchase records are kept because tax rules require it, but the email address attached to them can be removed from the list and from any future contact.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and no data is knowingly collected from them.

Changes

If this changes, the date below changes with it. Anything materially different will be said in an email to the list rather than quietly edited.
