Privacy

What this site collects, why, and how to have it removed.

Gazillion Industries is a one-person company that makes audio plug-ins. Nothing here is sold, rented or shared with data brokers, and there is no advertising network running on this site beyond what is listed below.

What the site itself collects

Two things run on these pages, and both are described honestly below rather than buried.

ServiceWhat it doesWhat it holds
Umami Counts page visits and which buttons are pressed, so it is possible to tell whether a page works. No cookies and no cross-site tracking. Page address, referrer, country, browser and device type, and an anonymous daily identifier that cannot be traced back to a person or joined up across days.
Meta pixel Reports that a visit or a checkout happened, so advertising on Facebook and Instagram can be measured and targeted. Meta receives the page visited and, if a purchase is started, the product and price. Meta may connect that to your Facebook or Instagram account. This one is genuinely advertising tracking, and if you would rather it did not happen, a tracker blocker or Meta's own ad preferences will stop it.

What happens when you buy something

Payment is handled entirely by Gumroad. Card details are never sent to this site and are never seen by Gazillion Industries. What comes back is your email address, the product, the amount, and the country the order came from, which is what a receipt and a licence need. Gumroad's own privacy policy covers their side.

What happens if you join the email list

The list runs on Kit. It holds the email address you gave, and which emails you opened or clicked, which is how sending stays occasional rather than constant. Every email carries an unsubscribe link and it works immediately.

The plug-ins themselves

DRUGS and F(ilter)12 do not phone home. They contain no analytics, send nothing anywhere, and work with no internet connection. What you make with them is yours and is never transmitted.

Removing your data

Email tony@gazillionindustries.com and say what you want removed. No form and no account required. Purchase records are kept because tax rules require it, but the email address attached to them can be removed from the list and from any future contact.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and no data is knowingly collected from them.

Changes

If this changes, the date below changes with it. Anything materially different will be said in an email to the list rather than quietly edited.
